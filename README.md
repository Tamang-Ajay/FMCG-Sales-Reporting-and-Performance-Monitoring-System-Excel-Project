# 🛒 FMCG Sales Reporting and Performance Monitoring System
# <a href="#">View Project</a>

## 📌Overview:
I developed an Excel-based FMCG Sales Reporting and Performance Monitoring System consisting of three major components: Daily MIS Reporting, Annual Cumulative MIS Reporting, and an Interactive Visual Dashboard.
The system is designed to monitor daily sales performance, track monthly and cumulative annual achievements against assigned targets, and analyze overall business performance using interactive visual reports. The system provides insights into sales network-wise, zone-wise, and product category-wise performance, along with customer analysis and overall sales trends.

## 🎯Goal and 🚧Challenges:
The goal of this project was to develop a structured reporting system to:
- Monitor daily revenue and quantity sold against assigned targets.
- Analyze daily sales performance through Sales Network-wise, Zone-wise, and Product Category-wise reporting.
- Track monthly revenue, quantity sold, and Year-to-Date (YTD) performance against annual targets.
- Monitor key business indicators including Total Sales, Quantity Sold, Invoice Count, Average Invoice Value, and Unique Customers.
- Identify top-performing customers, sales networks, and products.
- Support business monitoring and informed decision-making through interactive visual analysis.
<br><B>The</B> challenges involved organizing daily sales transaction data, maintaining consistency between daily and annual MIS reports, and connecting the consolidated dataset to an interactive dashboard. Ensuring accurate calculations, cumulative target tracking, and automatic updates of dashboard visualizations after refreshing the data connection were also important considerations.

## 🔧 Key Features:
### 1.Daily MIS Report
- Daily Sales Monitoring: Tracked daily revenue and quantity sold against assigned targets.
- Performance Analysis: Monitored sales network-wise, zone-wise, and product category-wise performance.
### 2.Annual Cumulative MIS Report
- Monthly Performance Tracking: Monitored monthly revenue and quantity sold.
- YTD Analysis: Tracked cumulative performance against annual targets.
### 3.Interactive Visual Dashboard
- Dynamic Data Updates: Updated KPIs and visualizations by refreshing the consolidated sales dataset.
- KPI Cards: Displayed Total Sales, Quantity Sold, Invoice Count, Average Invoice Value, and Unique Customers.
- Sales Analysis: Visualized monthly and quarterly trends, regional performance, payment types, and top-performing customers, networks, and products.
- Interactive Filters & Slicers: Enabled analysis by Sales Network Type, Quarter, Region, and Product Category.

## 💡Impact:
- Provided a structured approach to daily and annual FMCG sales reporting.
- Made it easier to monitor sales performance across different business segments.
- Improved visibility into regional, product, and sales network performance.
- Demonstrated how Microsoft Excel can transform transaction-level data into meaningful MIS reports and business insights.
